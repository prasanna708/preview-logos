Level Up · Personalised Fitness Studio · Round 3

Identity concepts

Five directions, each presented the way a finished identity is delivered: the primary logo, a one-colour version on the brand colour, a second layout, an app icon and a small palette.

01

Lift

LEVEL begins and ends with L. The two L's lock together into a square, and the second one is lifted a step, which turns the square into LEVEL UP. The corners also read as a focus frame, for training built around one person.

02

Climb

LEVEL runs along the baseline, then UP turns and climbs. The lockup traces the idea itself: along the level, then up. Set in the black and red of the GymMobile app.

03

Split Level

The wordmark is cut at mid-height and the top half is lifted and pushed forward, like a jump caught in motion. It uses one colour only, so it prints cleanly on kit, walls and equipment, with Volt as the supporting colour.

04

Boutique

A quieter, premium direction for a members-style studio. The lettering has high-waisted E's, a small nod to lifting, and the seal carries an LU monogram in which the U sits a step higher than the L.

05

You

The U of UP stands for you. A dot lifted clear of it is the client, raised up, and the whole shape also reads as someone with both arms in the air.

These are concepts for discussion. The chosen direction will be refined and delivered as a full logo set: horizontal, stacked, symbol-only and one-colour versions, in SVG and PNG.
