Personalised Fitness Studio · Round 2

Level Up logo concepts

Six new ideas. None of them reuse the arrows, dumbbells or athlete figures from your reference logos. Each is shown on the two backgrounds from the GymMobile app theme, then tested at app-icon size.

Press Up

A game-controller D-pad with the up button lit. The same shape reads as a health cross, and a press-up is the British name for a push-up.

Box Jump

Three plyo boxes of rising height, with a client mid-jump onto the tallest. The hand-hold slots make them read as boxes rather than a bar chart.

Outfit ExtraBold, lowercase · Teal

Hero black #0F0F0F
Scaffold #F5F5F2
03

Contour

Contour lines climbing to a summit, as on a topographic map. The same rings look like a fingerprint, which suits a personalised studio.

Jost Medium, wide tracking · Cobalt

Hero black #0F0F0F
Scaffold #F5F5F2
04

Scoreboard

LEVEL UP on a dot-matrix display, like the interval timer on a gym wall or the level-up screen in an arcade game. Red LEDs to match your app.

Dot-matrix lettering, Space Mono · App Red

Hero black #0F0F0F
Scaffold #F5F5F2
05

Exponent

UP set as a superscript, so the word itself sits up. It also suggests progress that compounds, like a number raised to a power.

Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold · Violet

Hero black #0F0F0F
Scaffold #F5F5F2
06

Signature

A jersey-style script with a tail swept under the name. A signature is personal by definition, and the script feels friendlier than block capitals.

Yellowtail script · Amber
