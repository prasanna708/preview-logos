Level Up · Personalised Fitness Studio · Round 4

Icon logos

Twelve logos led by a symbol. Each icon works on its own, on a sign, a T-shirt or as an app icon, and pairs with the custom LEVEL UP lettering.

01 Pull-Up 02 Step Up 03 Kettlebell Peak 04 25 kg 05 Summit 06 Ignite 07 Hex Dumbbell 08 Stopwatch 09 Medal 10 Heavy Bag 11 Rings 12 Pulse
01

Pull-Up

A pull-up at the top of the rep, chin over the bar. The bar is the level, and the head rising above it is the client levelling up.

02

Step Up

A step-up, one of the first moves in any personal programme, with the platforms rising like levels. The figure is drawn in the style of sports pictograms, so it stays clear at small sizes.

03

Kettlebell Peak

A kettlebell whose handle opening is a triangle pointing up, so the most recognisable piece of studio kit also says up. The handle carries the accent colour, and the solid shape stays bold on signage and embroidery.

04

25 kg

A red competition bumper plate. In competition colours, red marks the 25 kg plate, the heaviest standard disc, and the plate carries the studio name the way real equipment does. It uses the red from the GymMobile app.

05

Summit

A peak with a switchback trail and a flag at the top. Each turn in the trail is a level, and the flag is the goal a client sets with their coach. Paired with the lighter lettering for a calmer, premium feel.

06

Ignite

A two-tone flame for energy and effort. It is the most direct of the six and reads instantly at any size, from a phone icon to the studio wall.

07

Hex Dumbbell

A hex dumbbell tilted upward, with the rising end in amber. The most familiar object in any gym, turned so that it climbs.

08

Stopwatch

A stopwatch with the hand at twelve and a progress ring running round the dial. Every session is timed and tracked, and the ring fills as the client moves up.

09

Medal

A gold medal for a personal best, the result every client is working towards. A classic badge that reads well on kit and on social posts.

10

Heavy Bag

A heavy bag for boxing and conditioning work. Simple shapes that stay clear on a sign, a pair of gloves or a phone screen.

11

Rings

Gymnastic rings hanging from a beam, for bodyweight strength. The beam is the level, and the rings are where clients pull themselves up.

12

Pulse

A heart whose pulse line steps up a level after the beat. It suits a studio that puts health and steady progress first.

These are concepts for discussion. The chosen icon will be refined and delivered with the full logo set: icon only, horizontal, stacked and one-colour versions, in SVG and PNG, plus app launcher icons.
